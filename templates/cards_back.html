This card shows domain-specific statistics of Loom records based on research articles: the number of articles, authors, and statements. A statement is a natural language formulation of data analysis results. An article and related statements can belong, and an author can contribute, to more than one domain; therefore, the sum of counts in all domains is not equal to the Overall count.
To discover more on this topic or find specific information, visit the Loom search page.

This card shows domain-specific statistics of data (CSV) and code (Python or R) files currently available as supplementary materials to Loom records. A record with these files can belong to more than one domain; therefore, the sum of counts in all domains is not equal to the Overall count.
The files can be directly accessed and downloaded via the  Detailed Information section.

This card shows domain-specific statistics of schemata applied in Loom records. Schemata are registered and persistently identified data types. Loom schemata cover most widely used statistical and machine learning methods. As a record that includes schemata can belong to more than one domain, the sum of counts in all domains is not equal to the Overall count.
In the figure, selecting a schema to report a data analysis method is explained (click the image to expand it). To know more about the schemata, visit the Loom help page.
